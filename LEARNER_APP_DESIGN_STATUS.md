## Design System Applied

### Colors
- **Primary**: Purple gradients (from-purple-500 to-purple-700)
- **Secondary**: Blue, Green, Orange gradients
- **Success**: Green gradients
- **Warning**: Orange/Red gradients
- **Backgrounds**: Light gradients (slate/purple/pink-50)
- **Dark Backgrounds**: Deep gradients (indigo/purple/pink-900)

### Typography
- **Headings**: text-4xl to text-6xl, font-bold
- **Body**: text-lg to text-2xl
- **Buttons**: text-xl to text-3xl

### Spacing
- **Padding**: p-6 to p-12 (responsive)
- **Margins**: mb-6 to mb-12
- **Gaps**: gap-4 to gap-8

### Shadows
- **Cards**: shadow-lg to shadow-2xl
- **Buttons**: shadow-md to shadow-xl
- **Hover**: Increase shadow on hover

### Borders
- **Cards**: border border-neutral-100 or border-white/20
- **Inputs**: border-2 with color variants
- **Rounded**: rounded-xl to rounded-3xl

### Animations
- **Float**: Background elements (20-30s infinite)
- **Fade-in**: Content entrance (0.6-0.8s)
- **Slide-up**: Cards staggered entrance
- **Bounce**: Emoji icons
- **Pulse**: Recording indicators
- **Scale**: Hover effects (1.02-1.10)
- **Shake**: Error states
