## 🏆 Competitor Keyword Analysis

### Top Competitors

**ABCmouse:**
- Primary keywords: kids learning, preschool, kindergarten
- Strength: Brand recognition
- Weakness: Not specialized for neurodiverse learners

**Khan Academy Kids:**
- Primary keywords: education, math, reading, free
- Strength: Trusted brand, free
- Weakness: Not adaptive for special needs

**Endless Reader:**
- Primary keywords: reading, phonics, sight words, dyslexia
- Strength: Dyslexia focus
- Weakness: Limited subjects

**Otsimo:**
- Primary keywords: autism, special education, aac
- Strength: Autism specialization
- Weakness: Limited to autism spectrum

### AIVO's Competitive Advantage Keywords
- **AI-powered personalization** (unique)
- **Multi-disability support** (autism + ADHD + dyslexia)
- **Comprehensive curriculum** (not single-subject)
- **Parent & teacher dashboards** (family collaboration)
- **Accessibility-first design** (WCAG AAA compliance)

## 🎯 Seasonal Keyword Strategy

### Back to School (August - September)
- Add keywords: "back to school", "homework help", "learning support"
- Update promotional text with school-focused messaging
- Create screenshots showing homework assistance

### Holiday Season (November - December)
- Add keywords: "gift for kids", "educational gift", "learning app"
- Emphasize family features in description
- Highlight parent dashboard capabilities

### Summer Learning (June - July)
- Add keywords: "summer learning", "prevent learning loss", "summer skills"
- Update screenshots to show fun, engaging activities
- Emphasize screen time with educational value

### New Year (January)
- Add keywords: "new year goals", "learning goals", "skill building"
- Highlight goal-setting features
- Show progress tracking capabilities

## 🚫 Keywords to Avoid

**Blacklisted Keywords:**
- Competitor brand names (trademark infringement)
- Medical terms (we're not a medical device)
- Cure/treatment claims (FDA violations)
- Superlatives without proof ("best", "#1")
- Irrelevant trending keywords (keyword stuffing)

**Risky Keywords:**
- "therapy" (implies medical treatment)
- "diagnosis" (we don't diagnose)
- "medication" (we're not medical)
- "cure" (false claims)
