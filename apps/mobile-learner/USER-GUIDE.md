## Getting Started

### Download the App

**iOS (iPhone/iPad):**
1. Open the App Store
2. Search for "Aivo Learning"
3. Tap "Get" to download
4. Open the app

**Android (Phone/Tablet):**
1. Open the Google Play Store
2. Search for "Aivo Learning"
3. Tap "Install"
4. Open the app

### First-Time Setup

#### Step 1: Create a Free Account

1. Open Aivo Learning
2. Tap "Get Started"
3. Choose "Parent" or "Student" (parents should set up the account)
4. Enter your email address
5. Create a secure password
6. Verify your email

#### Step 2: Set Up Student Profile

1. Enter your child's first name
2. Select their grade level (K-5, Middle School, or High School)
3. Choose learning preferences (optional)
4. Take a quick assessment (helps us personalize lessons)

#### Step 3: Customize Accessibility

If your child needs accessibility features:

1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility**
2. Enable features your child needs:
   - **Text-to-Speech**: App reads text aloud
   - **Voice Input**: Speak answers instead of typing
   - **Dyslexia Font**: Special font for easier reading
   - **Larger Text**: Increase text size
   - **High Contrast**: Easier to see
   - **Reduce Motion**: Less animation

3. Test the settings with your child

Access your parent dashboard from the **Progress** tab to see:

- Time spent learning

🎯 **Skill Mastery**

📈 **Detailed Reports**

### Setting Learning Goals

1. Go to **Settings** > **Goals**
2. Tap "Add New Goal"
3. Choose:
   - Subject (Math, Reading, etc.)
   - Skill to improve
   - Target date
   - Daily practice time
4. Track progress in the Progress tab

### Managing Screen Time

**Set Daily Time Limits:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Parental Controls**
2. Set daily learning time (e.g., 30 minutes)
3. Choose break reminders (every 15 minutes)
4. App will notify when limit is reached

**Scheduled Learning Times:**
1. Set specific times for learning (e.g., 4:00 PM - 4:30 PM)
2. App sends reminders
3. Flexible for weekends

### Homework Helper Tips

The **Homework Helper** uses AI to assist your child:

✅ **Best Practices:**
- Encourage your child to try problems first
- Use AI for hints, not complete answers
- Review solutions together
- AI explains step-by-step

⚠️ **What Parents Should Know:**
- AI responses are age-appropriate
- No ads or inappropriate content
- All conversations are monitored for safety
- You can review homework help history

**How to Use:**
1. Your child takes a photo of homework
2. AI recognizes the problem
3. Child asks for help
4. AI provides hints and explanations
5. Child works through the solution
6. AI confirms if correct

### Communication with Teachers

**Share Progress:**
1. Go to **Progress** > **Reports**
2. Tap **Export PDF**
3. Email to teacher or bring to IEP meeting

**Invite Teacher:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Connect Teacher**
2. Enter teacher's email
3. Teacher receives secure invite
4. They can view progress (read-only)

## Accessibility Features

### 🔊 Text-to-Speech

**Have Aivo Read to You:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility**
2. Turn on **Text-to-Speech**
3. Adjust reading speed (slow, normal, fast)
4. Adjust voice pitch
5. Now tap any text to hear it read aloud!

**Quick Tip:** Tap the 🔊 speaker icon on any screen for instant read-aloud.

### 🎤 Voice Input

**Speak Your Answers:**
1. Enable **Voice Input** in Accessibility settings
2. When answering a question, tap the 🎤 microphone button
3. Speak clearly
4. AI converts your speech to text
5. Tap submit!

**Great for:**
- Students who struggle with typing
- Verbal learners
- Hands-free learning

### 👓 Dyslexia-Friendly Font

**Easier Reading:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility**
2. Turn on **Dyslexia Font**
3. Text changes to OpenDyslexic font
4. Letters are clearer and more distinct
5. Reduces letter confusion (b/d, p/q)

### 📏 Text Scaling

**Make Text Bigger:**
1. Go to **Settings** > **Accessibility**
2. Drag the **Text Size** slider
3. Choose from 80% to 200%
4. Preview shows real-time changes
5. App updates throughout

### 🌓 High Contrast Mode

**See Better:**
1. Enable **High Contrast** in Accessibility
2. Colors become more distinct
3. Easier to see buttons and text
4. Reduces eye strain

### 🐢 Reduce Motion

**Less Animation:**
1. Turn on **Reduce Motion**
2. Animations slow down or stop
3. Helpful for motion sensitivity
4. Reduces visual distractions

### 📳 Haptic Feedback

**Feel Interactions:**
1. Enable **Haptic Feedback**
2. Phone vibrates when you:
   - Press buttons
   - Answer correctly
   - Get achievements
   - Complete lessons
3. Helps with sensory feedback

### ♿️ Screen Reader Support

**For Blind/Low Vision:**
- **iOS**: VoiceOver automatically supported
- **Android**: TalkBack automatically supported
- Every button and element is labeled
- Navigate with swipe gestures
- Hear everything on screen

## Troubleshooting

### App Won't Open

**Try this:**
1. Force close the app
2. Restart your device
3. Check for app updates in App Store/Play Store
4. Reinstall app (your data is saved in the cloud)

### Forgot Password

1. On login screen, tap "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check email for reset link
4. Create new password
5. Log in with new password

### Lessons Won't Load

**Check:**
- Are you connected to internet?
- Is offline mode enabled? (lessons must be downloaded first)
- Try closing and reopening the app
- Check your internet connection

**Still not working?**
- Contact support@aivolearning.com

### Progress Not Syncing

**Fix:**
1. Make sure you're online
2. Go to **Settings** > **Sync**
3. Tap "Sync Now"
4. Wait for sync to complete

### Voice Input Not Working

**Troubleshooting:**
1. Check microphone permissions:
   - **iOS**: Settings > Aivo Learning > Microphone (ON)
   - **Android**: Settings > Apps > Aivo Learning > Permissions > Microphone (Allow)
2. Speak clearly near the microphone
3. Reduce background noise
4. Try restarting the app

### Screen Reader Issues

**VoiceOver (iOS):**
1. Enable VoiceOver: Settings > Accessibility > VoiceOver
2. Navigate with swipe gestures
3. Double-tap to activate
4. If issues, restart app with VoiceOver enabled

**TalkBack (Android):**
1. Enable TalkBack: Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack
2. Navigate with swipe gestures
3. Double-tap to activate
4. If issues, restart app with TalkBack enabled

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Aivo work offline?

**Yes!** Download lessons when online, then use them anywhere. Progress saves locally and syncs when you're back online.

### How much does Aivo cost?

**Free to start!** Basic features are free forever. Premium features (advanced AI tutoring, unlimited homework help) available with subscription.

### What ages is Aivo for?

**K-12 students** (ages 5-18), with special focus on students with learning differences like ADHD, Autism, and Dyslexia.

### Can multiple children use one account?

**Not yet**, but we're working on it! For now, create separate accounts for each child.

### Does Aivo replace teachers?

**No!** Aivo is a supplement to classroom learning. It helps with practice, homework, and review. Always work with your child's teacher.

### Is Aivo IEP-friendly?

**Yes!** Progress tracking aligns with IEP goals. Export reports for IEP meetings. Many features designed for special education.

### What if my child gets frustrated?

**Built-in breaks!** The app reminds students to take breaks. Difficulty automatically adjusts. Encouraging messages throughout. If frustration persists, lower the difficulty in settings.

### Can I use Aivo at school?

**Check with your school!** Many schools allow educational apps. Some have classroom licenses. Contact us for school/district pricing.
