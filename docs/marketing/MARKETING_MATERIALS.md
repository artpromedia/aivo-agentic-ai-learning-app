## Launch Campaign

### Pre-Launch (2 weeks before)

**Week -2:**
- Announce launch date on social media
- Create teaser content (sneak peeks)
- Reach out to education bloggers/influencers
- Submit press release to education media
- Set up landing page with email capture

**Week -1:**
- Daily countdown posts
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Founder Q&A video
- Beta tester testimonials
- Final press kit distribution

### Launch Day

**Activities:**
- Press release distribution (8am)
- Social media blitz (scheduled posts)
- Email announcement to waitlist
- Product Hunt launch
- Reddit r/specialed post
- Facebook groups outreach
- Twitter thread with feature demos

**Content:**
- Hero announcement post (all platforms)
- Feature highlight thread
- Testimonial posts
- Demo video premiere
- Live Q&A session

**Week 1:**
- Thank you posts to early adopters
- Share user reviews and testimonials
- Behind-the-scenes content
- App tips and tricks
- Respond to all reviews

**Week 2-4:**
- Weekly feature spotlight posts
- Parent success stories
- Teacher tips
- Accessibility awareness content
- Community building
